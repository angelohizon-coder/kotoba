## Primary pacing: Nihongo Sō-matome

The [ASK series overview](https://ask-books.com/somatome/) describes short daily work and weekly consolidation. Its overview still says six weeks, while the **expanded/revised N3, N2 and N1 volumes linked below each advertise two pages a day over eight weeks, with Day 7 review**. These are edition-specific publisher descriptions, not an interchangeable six/eight-week claim.

Kotoba adapts the short-study/review rhythm: six study lessons followed by cumulative review, at most seven new words and two grammar patterns per study lesson. Its unit count follows the available course banks. The app's prerequisite order, daily word/pattern combinations, review questions and context sentences are original; its lessons are not identified with a publisher's Day 1, Chapter 1 or page range.

## Context, progression and coverage limits

Kotoba's daily context sentences literally use the selected vocabulary examples and grammar examples. A longer original reading or dialogue is an additional skill task; it is not presented as containing every word or construction from that lesson.

All five levels have integrated paths combining words, linked kanji, grammar, original reading/listening and cumulative retrieval. N5/N4 follow the foundation form sequence; N3, N2 and N1 group grammar by prerequisite families. The paths revisit weak answers and offer original full mocks. The [official level summaries](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/about/levelsummary.html) describe increasing comprehension demands, without providing an exhaustive word/grammar syllabus.

The expansion preserves every established N3–N1 lesson ID, target, context and question check, then appends separate extra-study units. The original revision is identified as **Earlier library revision and mock** and keeps its earlier content; a separate full-library revision follows the additions. This preserves what saved completion marks mean while giving new material its own lessons and assessment links.

Legacy fixed six-week topic-plan data remains only to validate older saves. The current interface uses the variable-length learning path; topics are browsing tags. No fixed topic sequence is asserted to match Sou-matome chapters or a current eight-week volume.

The representative kanji links teach readings through vocabulary words. They do not reproduce any publisher's full character or reading inventory or establish stroke-order coverage. Full commercial books were not accessed; completing an app path does not certify completion of a book or an exhaustive JLPT syllabus.

The app's N3 listening-expression questions use written situations as an adaptation. Device speech depends on Japanese voices installed in the browser or operating system; it does not reproduce textbook recordings or official picture-based questions. Publisher audio and [official listening materials](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html) remain separate resources.

The expanded N2/N1 listening bank now includes original dedicated quick-response exercises alongside tasks, key points, outline and integrated comprehension. These use the existing device-speech player, with scripts and explanations available under the established assessment rules. Catalogue descriptions of the listening formats do not imply that the app reproduces official audio or a complete textbook. Human-recorded dialogue, expert review of linguistic nuance and listening difficulty, and precise publisher item/chapter/page mapping remain outstanding.

## Interface and publishing references

Duolingo's [core-tabs redesign](https://blog.duolingo.com/core-tabs-redesign/) informed consistent navigation, rounded cards and cheerful visual hierarchy. Kotoba's mascot, tones, branding and assets are original. The home screen starts with the learning path, daily goal and review queue; optional widgets can be shown, hidden and reordered. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences, and written feedback remains available when sounds are muted.

Deployment follows [GitHub Pages' static hosting and public-repository availability](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and its [custom workflow guide](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/using-custom-workflows-with-github-pages). The default host is GitHub's github.io address, with relative assets and hash routes. No recurring paid service or custom domain is required.
